# 🎓 Bachelor’s Thesis – “CuidaMe”

## Alexa Skill for Supporting People with Cognitive and Physical Limitations
As part of my Bachelor’s Thesis, I developed “CuidaMe”, an Alexa skill designed to assist elderly people and individuals with cognitive and/or physical limitations in managing and completing their daily tasks independently and engagingly.

The project aims to enhance autonomy and accessibility through a multimodal application that combines voice and touch interaction on Alexa devices such as the Echo and Echo Show. Following ISO standards for accessibility, usability, and user-centered design (UX), the app delivers a simple and inclusive experience adapted to the needs of vulnerable users.

“CuidaMe” offers two user profiles:

👤 End User: interacts through voice or touch to access a weekly calendar, receive task reminders, and confirm task completion. Users can choose between two virtual avatars that guide them through the interface, complete weekly cognitive (Mini-Mental) and satisfaction questionnaires, view their progress in charts and messages, and select a reward (music, YouTube video, or a color-sequence memory game) for each completed task. They can also converse naturally with an AI chat assistant for questions or motivation.

🧑‍⚕️ Caregiver: manages the user’s activities, with the ability to create, view, and delete tasks, and automatically receives the results of cognitive questionnaires by email.

Technically, the skill supports voice activation and closure, stores user information securely, and ensures data privacy under the GDPR and Alexa privacy standards. The virtual guide (2D avatar) appears on key screens to enhance understanding, and all communications are handled securely over HTTPS.

### Tech stack:
AWS Lambda • Alexa Skills Kit • Node.js • Alexa Presentation Language (APL) • Firebase • Amazon S3
